Delete your account

Applies to organizer accounts in the Evento app.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Evento and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You're signed out immediately.

If you don't sign back in within 30 days, your account — name, email, and phone number — is permanently deleted. Signing back in before then automatically cancels the request; nothing further to do. Events you organized are left as published for guests who already joined, until those events finish their own normal 30-day retention window — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Option 2 — Without the app

Don't have the app installed, or can't sign in? Email support@jtdigitals.com from any address, with:

  • Subject: Delete my account
  • The phone number your organizer account is registered under

We'll verify the request and process the deletion within 30 days, the same as requesting it in-app. The same retention rules above apply — events you organized stay visible to guests who already joined until their own normal retention window ends.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email, and phone number are removed from our systems.
  • Your sign-in credential is deleted — the account can't be signed into again.

Events you organized, their guest lists, and submitted photos are not deleted as part of this — they're left intact for any guests who already joined, and expire on their own normal 30-day retention schedule, independent of your account.
