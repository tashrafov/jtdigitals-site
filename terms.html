1. What Evento does

Evento lets an organizer create a time-boxed photo-sharing event. Guests join using a QR code or join code, capture a limited ("capped") number of photos through the app, and submit them. After a review window set by the organizer, the submitted photos are combined into a shared gallery made available to the event's guests and organizer.
