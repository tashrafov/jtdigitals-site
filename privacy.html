Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JT Digitals ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Evento mobile application and the jtdigitals.com website (together, the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account and verification data: such as a phone number used to verify an organizer before they can create an event.
  • Event and guest data: event names, join codes, guest display names, and the photos guests capture and submit through an event.
  • Device and usage data: such as app version, device type, and basic diagnostic/crash information, used to keep the Service reliable.
  • Push notification tokens: used to notify you about event activity, such as when a gallery is published.

2. How we use information

  • To operate core features: creating events, joining events, capturing and submitting photos, and publishing shared galleries.
  • To send push notifications related to events you are part of (for example, that a gallery is ready).
  • To maintain the security of the Service, including preventing abuse of join codes or event access.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

3. How information is shared

  • With other guests and the organizer of the same event: once an event's review window closes, submitted photos become part of a shared gallery visible to that event's organizer and guests.
  • With service providers: we use infrastructure providers (such as Firebase, for authentication, data storage, and push notifications) to operate the Service. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of JT Digitals, our users, or others.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

We retain event data, including submitted photos, for as long as needed to provide the Service to the organizer and guests of that event, and as reasonably needed for backups, legal, or security purposes. Organizers may be able to delete an event they created; this may remove the associated gallery for all guests.

5. Your choices, including account deletion

  • You can decline to grant camera or notification permissions, though doing so will limit core functionality of the Service.
  • Deleting your account: organizers can request deletion directly in the app, from Profile → Delete account. You're signed out right away. If you don't sign back in within 30 days, your account — including your name, email, and phone number — is permanently deleted. Signing back in before then automatically cancels the request, with nothing further to do. Events you organized are left as published for guests who already joined either way, so a shared gallery isn't pulled out from under people who still have a legitimate reason to see it — those galleries simply run out their normal 30-day retention window on their own (see Section 4). You can also request deletion without the app installed — see Delete your account.
  • You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to the same legitimate-business limitation above.

6. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the Service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live, including wherever our infrastructure providers operate servers. We take steps to protect information consistent with this Policy wherever it is processed.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal information, can be sent to support@jtdigitals.com.
